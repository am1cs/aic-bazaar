## 0:00–0:20 — Who we are and the problem

Hi, we are [name] and [name], Astana IT University graduates building AIC Bazaar.
Businesses commission AI content, but both sides face a payment problem: creators
risk unpaid work, while customers risk paying for unfinished delivery.

## 0:20–0:55 — What the product does

AIC Bazaar puts an agreed brief into a funded deal on Solana. The customer reserves
the full budget. The selected creator accepts the terms and posts a ten-percent
performance deposit. Our first template is a fifteen-second advertisement with
three stages: concept, draft, and final delivery. Acceptance releases each stage's
payment; completion also returns the deposit.

## 0:55–1:20 — Disagreement and the role of Solana

Creative quality needs human judgment. Either party can open a dispute, freezing
normal payments. A named arbiter reviews the brief and evidence, then pays the
current stage or returns the unused budget. A proven creator breach can forfeit
the deposit. The contract enforces these payouts and permissions. Earlier payments
cannot be reversed.

We plan to start with accessible Web3 teams and creators already using wallets.
Paid demand remains unvalidated. Next, we will test actual commissioning workflows,
improve acceptance criteria, and explore fees on completed work. We used AI-assisted
